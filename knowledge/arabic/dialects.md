# Palestinian Sub-Dialects (דיאלקטים פלסטיניים)

> Regional variations within Palestinian Arabic.
> Helps Claude choose the right dialect for context.

## Overview

Palestinian Arabic is not one dialect — it varies significantly by region.
Key dialect groups:

1. **ירושלמי (القدسية)** — Jerusalem and surroundings
2. **חברוני (الخليلية)** — Hebron area, known for distinctive pronunciation
3. **נאבלוסי (النابلسية)** — Nablus area
4. **צפוני/גלילי (الجليلية)** — Galilee, often influenced by Hebrew
5. **עזתי (الغزاوية)** — Gaza, some Egyptian influence
6. **בדואי (البدوية)** — Bedouin dialects (Negev, Jordan Valley)
